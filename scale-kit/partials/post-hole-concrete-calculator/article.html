How to Calculate Concrete for Fence Post Holes

Post holes are cylinders, so volume = π × radius² × depth. This calculator converts your hole diameter and depth into cubic feet per hole, multiplies by your number of holes, and divides by your bag's yield to give a total bag count.

Standard Hole Sizing Rule of Thumb

Post SizeHole DiameterHole Depth
4x4 post8-10 inches24-30 inches (or below frost line)
6x6 post12 inches30-36 inches

A common rule: hole depth should be at least 1/3 of the post's above-ground height, and always below your local frost line. Check your area's building code before digging.
