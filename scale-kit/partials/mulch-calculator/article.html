How Much Mulch Do I Need?

Mulch is sold both in bags (typically 2 or 3 cubic feet) and in bulk by the cubic yard. Multiply your bed's area by the desired depth to find total volume, then either divide by your bag size or convert to cubic yards for bulk delivery pricing.

Recommended Mulch Depth

Use CaseDepth
Flower beds2-3 inches
Around trees/shrubs3-4 inches
Weed suppression3 inches minimum

Bulk vs. Bagged Mulch

For areas larger than about 2 cubic yards, bulk delivery is almost always cheaper per cubic foot than buying bags, though bagged mulch is more convenient for small beds or touch-ups.
