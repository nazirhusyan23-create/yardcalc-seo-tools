How This Concrete Bag Calculator Works

The calculator multiplies your slab's length × width × thickness to get a raw volume in cubic feet, then divides that number by the yield of the bag size you select.

Tips Before You Buy

Always round up to the nearest full bag, and add one extra bag on small jobs to cover spillage or an uneven base.
