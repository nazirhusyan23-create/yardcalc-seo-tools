How to Calculate Gravel for a Driveway or Path

Multiply the area (length × width) by your desired depth to get volume in cubic feet, then convert to cubic yards (÷27) since gravel is usually sold by the yard or by weight in tons. Different gravel types have different densities, which is why tonnage varies even for the same volume.

Gravel Density Reference

Gravel weighs roughly 1.4 tons per cubic yard, but crushed stone, pea gravel, and river rock all differ slightly based on stone size and compaction. Suppliers usually price gravel by the ton, so use the tonnage figure when ordering.
