About YardCalc

YardCalc builds free, no-signup calculators for home improvement and construction projects — concrete, gravel, flooring, fencing, and more. The site started as a set of personal spreadsheets used to avoid over-ordering materials on small DIY jobs, and grew into a small library of calculators covering the most common yard and construction estimates.

How We Build Our Calculators

Every formula on this site is based on published manufacturer specifications and standard industry conversion figures — for example, concrete bag yields are taken from manufacturer data sheets, and material densities (gravel, mulch, topsoil) come from standard industry references. Where a figure can vary by brand or region, we note that on the calculator page and recommend confirming with your supplier.

Editorial Policy

Our tools are for general estimation only and are not a substitute for professional measurement or advice. We review and update the underlying figures whenever manufacturer specs or industry standards change.
