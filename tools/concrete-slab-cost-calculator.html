How to Estimate Concrete Slab Cost

This calculator finds your slab's volume in cubic yards, then multiplies by your local ready-mix price per cubic yard to give a materials-only estimate. Ready-mix delivery is typically cheaper per cubic foot than bagged concrete once you need more than roughly 1 cubic yard — use our concrete bag calculator to compare bagged pricing for smaller pours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does ready-mix concrete cost per cubic yard?

Prices vary by region, typically $120-180 per cubic yard for standard mix, plus delivery fees.

Does this estimate include labor?

No, this is a materials-only estimate. Labor for pouring and finishing typically adds $2-8 per square foot.

Is bagged or ready-mix concrete cheaper for a slab?

Ready-mix is usually cheaper per cubic foot once you need more than about 1 cubic yard of concrete.
