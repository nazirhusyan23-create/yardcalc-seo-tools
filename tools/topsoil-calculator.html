How Much Topsoil Do I Need?

Topsoil volume follows the same math as gravel or mulch: area × depth = volume. Topsoil is typically sold by the cubic yard in bulk, or in 0.75 cubic foot bags for small jobs. Because topsoil settles and compacts, many landscapers add 10-15% extra to the raw calculated volume.

Recommended Topsoil Depth

Use CaseDepth
New lawn (over existing soil)4-6 inches
Raised garden bed8-12 inches

Topsoil vs. Compost vs. Fill Dirt

Topsoil is nutrient-rich and meant for planting; fill dirt is cheaper and used purely for grading/leveling; compost is often blended in on top of topsoil for garden beds. Use this calculator's cubic yard figure regardless of which material you choose — volume math is identical.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much topsoil do I need for a new lawn?

Typically 4-6 inches spread over the existing grade, though this varies with soil quality and drainage needs.

How many tons is a cubic yard of topsoil?

Roughly 1.35 tons, though moisture content can shift this figure somewhat.

Should I add extra topsoil for settling?

Yes, adding 10-15% extra accounts for natural compaction after watering and time.
