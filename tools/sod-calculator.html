How Much Sod Do I Need for My Lawn?

Multiply lawn length by width to get square footage, then add a small waste allowance for irregular edges and trimming. Sod is sold both by the pallet (covering roughly 450 square feet) and as individual rolls or slabs (roughly 9 square feet each).

Sod Coverage Reference

UnitTypical Coverage
1 pallet~450-500 ft²
1 roll/slab~9 ft² (varies by farm)

Order sod as close to installation day as possible — it typically needs to be laid within 24-48 hours of harvest for best results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much sod is on one pallet?

Typically 450-500 square feet per pallet, though this varies slightly by sod farm.

How soon after delivery should sod be installed?

Ideally within 24-48 hours of harvest to prevent it from drying out or overheating in the roll.

How much extra sod should I order?

About 5% extra covers trimming waste around curves and edges.
