How Many Pavers Do I Need?

Divide your patio or walkway's total area by the area of a single paver, then add a waste allowance for cuts around edges and curves. A 10% allowance covers most rectangular layouts; complex patterns or curved borders may need 15-20%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much waste allowance should I add for pavers?

10% for simple rectangular layouts, and 15-20% for curved edges, diagonal patterns, or complex borders.

What base do I need under pavers?

A compacted gravel base of 4-6 inches plus a 1-inch bedding sand layer is standard for most patios and walkways.

Do I need polymeric sand for pavers?

Yes, polymeric sand fills the joints between pavers and helps lock them in place while resisting weeds and ants.
