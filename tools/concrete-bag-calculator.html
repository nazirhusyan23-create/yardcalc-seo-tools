How This Concrete Bag Calculator Works

The calculator multiplies your slab's length × width × thickness to get a raw volume in cubic feet, then divides that number by the yield of the bag size you select.

Tips Before You Buy

Always round up to the nearest full bag, and add one extra bag on small jobs to cover spillage or an uneven base.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many 80lb bags of concrete do I need per cubic yard?

About 45 bags, since one cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet and each 80lb bag yields roughly 0.6 cubic feet.

How much area does a 60lb bag of concrete cover?

Roughly 1.35 square feet at a 4-inch slab thickness, since a 60lb bag yields about 0.45 cubic feet of mixed concrete.

Should I add extra bags for waste?

Yes — most contractors add 5-10% extra for spillage, subgrade unevenness, and rounding.
