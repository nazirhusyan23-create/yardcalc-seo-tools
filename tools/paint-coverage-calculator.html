How Much Paint Do I Need?

Multiply your wall area by the number of coats you plan to apply, then divide by your paint's coverage rate (usually printed on the can, typically 350-400 sq ft per gallon for smooth walls). This calculator rounds up to the nearest quart so you don't come up short mid-job.

Typical Coverage by Surface

SurfaceCoverage per Gallon
Smooth drywall350-400 ft²
Textured/stucco walls250-300 ft²
Bare wood (first coat)200-300 ft²
Masonry/brick150-200 ft²

Dark color changes and unprimed surfaces often need a 3rd coat — adjust the "coats" field accordingly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much wall area does a gallon of paint cover?

Typically 350-400 square feet per coat on smooth drywall, though textured or porous surfaces cover less.

How many coats of paint do I need?

Two coats is standard for most color changes; three coats may be needed for dark colors or unprimed surfaces.

Should I buy extra paint for touch-ups?

Yes, buying one extra quart is common practice for future touch-ups and matching color later.
